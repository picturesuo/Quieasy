    Privacy

    Quieasy only reads pages after you turn it on. For each question it sends the question wording and answer choices to the AI service you chose, which runs the web searches, and nothing else: no cookies, passwords, page addresses, names or screenshots. Answers are cached in browser memory until the browser restarts. Removing Quieasy from the browser deletes its settings and saved keys.
